## Recommended positioning

**Primary audience:** creative leads, studios and AI companies hiring a creative technologist to turn emerging technology into compelling, usable outputs. Superside and the separately verified Luma description support this direction. Stephen's strongest evidence combines creative judgement with practical delivery: generative filmmaking, sustained 3D/motion production, reusable workflows and code-connected media.

**Strong adjacent audience:** small technology companies needing a hands-on designer across product, brand and launches. Solve's brief closely resembles the breadth of Stephen's UNCX remit. This deserves a visible route through the portfolio, even when the job title differs.

**Selective audiences:** design engineering roles where implementation can be demonstrated, and product design roles where problem framing and interaction decisions are central. Duvo and Granola require deeper engineering evidence; ElevenLabs requires convincing product judgement. Do not present all three as equally proven specialisms.

**Working value proposition:** Stephen turns complex and emerging technology into clear interfaces, finished media and reusable creative workflows, combining art direction with hands-on building.

The differentiation is the connection between disciplines, supported by distinct work. Quiver demonstrates generative direction and finishing. The UNCX Video & 3D System demonstrates repeatable production. Academy connects explanation, learning and design. Badger demonstrates product scoping and implementation. Unified Menu demonstrates interaction systems. Switch Studios establishes delivery within development teams. Solana Diary adds API-connected production with mandatory human approval.

The specialist limits matter: 20+ years across design and technology is not 20+ years of professional React. The documented work does not establish enterprise frontend scale, extensive measured user research, model research, or specialist voice-AI product expertise. AI-assisted building is credible when accompanied by Stephen's decisions and verification.

## Requirements for the redesign

1. **Homepage:** name the primary role and value immediately, with Norwich/remote availability. Show visual evidence from at least three different projects early: finished media, an interface and a reusable production workflow. The opening must communicate Stephen's breadth before any individual case dominates. Include obvious Work, About, CV and Contact routes.
2. **Case studies:** lead with an inspectable outcome, then the brief, Stephen's exact contribution, one difficult decision, a useful intermediate artifact and the result/status. Quiver needs generation decisions and finishing; Badger needs an implemented flow and verification; Menu needs hierarchy alternatives and clear implementation status; the motion system needs evidence of reuse. Show real feedback where recorded. Avoid substituting process diagrams for work.
3. **CV:** retain the factual employment record and existing role variants, changing project order and emphasis for each application. Link each major claim to its best evidence. Attribute every metric to its employer/project; keep beta, archived and implementation-on-hold labels. Keep the document readable as extracted text.
4. **Creative brief:** develop competing visual concepts around Stephen's range, judgement and working practice. A portrait is optional. Each concept must make both artistic craft and technical thinking visible without relying on one project, invented results or a list of software logos.
