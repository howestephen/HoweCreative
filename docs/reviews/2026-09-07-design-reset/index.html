The index becomes
the sculpture.

A monumental typographic composition made from the work itself. Every title is a route into evidence; depth and motion give the index a distinctive physical presence.

Composition diagram. Four titles indicate hierarchy; the production layout must accommodate all ten current projects and an explicit earlier-work section.

What happens

Focus brings a title forward and reveals a project preview within its letterforms. Selecting it moves through a typographic aperture into the case study. Each title also remains an ordinary accessible link.

What it means

Your complete body of work creates the visual identity. Typography, spatial composition and interaction become a functioning navigation system rather than an opening decoration.

What makes it credible

Use shallow title geometry, a single active preview and controlled camera movement. Long names, media cropping and phone legibility need dedicated design. Its strength is discovery; its largest risk is visual density.
