# MARKET_MATRIX  -  Per-Market Compliance Matrix

## Purpose (sketch)

Consolidates per-market legal, privacy, tax, and consumer-rights obligations when the project operates across multiple jurisdictions.

## Sections to populate when activated

- **Active markets:** list (e.g. UK, EU, US California, US other states, Brazil, Mexico)
- **Per-market matrix:** one row per market × obligation
  - Privacy regime (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA / LGPD / LFPDPPP / …)
  - Consumer-rights regime
  - Tax / VAT / sales-tax obligations
  - Consent mechanism required
  - Data residency constraint
  - Cross-border transfer mechanism (SCCs, DPF, other)
  - Localised legal copy required (ToS, PP)
  - Market-specific payment rules
  - Market-specific content/age rules
- **Transfer mechanisms:** EU→US (DPF, SCCs), UK→US, LatAm→EU
- **Localisation checklist:** per new market
